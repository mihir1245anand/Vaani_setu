# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1: Is Vaani-Setu an official government portal?
A: No, Vaani-Setu is an educational prototype and conversational interface designed to help citizens easily navigate and discover eligible government schemes.

### Q2: Does the voice assistant work offline?
A: Speech synthesis works offline using local browser voices. Speech recognition depends on the browser engine (Web Speech API); on Chromium-based browsers, it seamlessly defaults to text input when offline.

### Q3: How are scheme eligibility criteria matched?
A: Criteria such as age, gender, occupation, annual income, land holding size, and state of residence are matched against scheme eligibility definitions.
