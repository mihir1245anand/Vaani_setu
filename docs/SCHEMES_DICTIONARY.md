# Government Schemes Data Dictionary

## Supported Scheme Categories
- **Agriculture & Farmers**: PM-Kisan Samman Nidhi, PM Fasal Bima Yojana, Soil Health Card.
- **Healthcare & Wellness**: Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY), Jan Aushadhi Yojana.
- **Housing & Urban/Rural Infrastructure**: PM Awas Yojana (PMAY-G & PMAY-U).
- **Financial Inclusion & Pension**: PM Jan Dhan Yojana, Atal Pension Yojana (APY), PM Suraksha Bima Yojana.
- **Women & Child Welfare**: PM Matru Vandana Yojana, Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana.
- **Skill Development & Employment**: PM Kaushal Vikas Yojana, PM SVANidhi (Street Vendors).

Each scheme record includes target demographics, maximum income thresholds, state restrictions, required documents, and application links.
